## The pitch

> PuddlePal uses satellite imagery and AI to measure every puddle on your street in real time, so you can step in each one with total confidence, for just $3.99 a month.

## How it works

1. Satellites photograph your street from orbit, around the clock.
2. Our AI measures every puddle — depth, width, splash potential — in real time.
3. PuddlePal sends the measurements straight to your phone, so you can step in each puddle with total confidence.

No puddle goes unmeasured. No step goes unplanned. No one asked for this.

## Why it exists

Because knowing the exact depth of a puddle before you step in it changes everything. Probably.

> "People have been stepping in puddles blind for centuries. We fixed that." — the founder, with total certainty, about puddles.

## Pricing

- Free tier: one puddle per day, measured with love.
- Premium ($3.99/month): unlimited puddles, real-time splash alerts, and a leaderboard of your wettest walks.
